## New Zealand SP Grower Visit

<img src="/images/new_zealand_visit.jpg" alt="New Zealand sweetpotato grower delegation visit" style="width:100%; max-width:850px; border-radius:10px;">

During a visit from the New Zealand sweetpotato grower delegation, our team presented the robot design concept and demonstrated its field locomotion and precision pesticide-spraying capabilities.

The demonstration highlighted the potential of legged robots for precision agriculture, especially in crop environments where conventional ground vehicles may damage vegetation.

## Guest Lecture for E102

<img src="/images/teaching.jpg" alt="Guest lecture for E102" style="width:100%; max-width:850px; border-radius:10px;">

As part of an E102 guest lecture, I introduced agricultural robotics, legged robot design, and the role of automation in future food-production systems.

## CALS Tailgate Outreach

<img src="/images/cals_tailgate.jpg" alt="CALS Tailgate robotics outreach" style="width:100%; max-width:850px; border-radius:10px;">

At the 2024 CALS Tailgate outreach event, our team presented legged robots through live demonstrations for children, families, and visitors.

The activity introduced basic robotics concepts and showed how robotic systems can support agricultural automation and field applications.
