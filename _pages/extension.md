At the 2025 Northeastern Robotics Conference (NERC), AgHexaNav was demonstrated as part of the poster and robot demo session. The conference featured more than 100 robotics research projects from the region and was hosted at Cornell University.

Cornell Chronicle and Cornell Bowers CIS featured the robot in their conference coverage. The article introduced AgHexaNav as a spider-like hexapod robot designed to navigate dense vine crops, survey plants, water seedlings, and administer herbicides while minimizing plant damage.

[Read the Cornell Chronicle article](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2025/10/droids-descend-cornell-robotics-conference)  
[Read the Cornell Bowers CIS article](https://bowers.cornell.edu/news-stories/droids-descend-cornell-robotics-conference)

## New Zealand SP Grower Visit

During a visit from the New Zealand sweetpotato grower delegation, our team presented the robot design concept and demonstrated its field locomotion and precision pesticide-spraying capabilities.

The demonstration highlighted the potential of legged robots for precision agriculture, especially in crop environments where conventional ground vehicles may damage vegetation.

## Media Coverage: Spider Robot for Cucumber Disease Monitoring

<img src="/images/spider_robot_media.jpg" alt="Spider robot for cucumber disease monitoring" style="width:100%; max-width:850px; border-radius:10px;">

NC State Sustainability featured our spider-like hexapod robot for cucumber disease monitoring. The article introduced the robot prototype and its potential role in carrying a vacuum spore trap through cucumber fields where wheeled robots may become tangled in vines.

[Read the article](https://sustainability.ncsu.edu/blog/2024/07/18/spider-robot-to-help-untangle-cucumber-disease/)

## CALS Tailgate Outreach

<img src="/images/cals_tailgate.jpg" alt="CALS Tailgate robotics outreach" style="width:100%; max-width:850px; border-radius:10px;">
